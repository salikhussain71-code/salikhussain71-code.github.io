Profile

I'm an 18-year-old student from Rawalpindi, Pakistan, currently pursuing my BS in Computer Science at Iqra University Islamabad. My research direction is low-resource multilingual NLP — specifically building production-grade AI systems for Urdu and Pakistani government information infrastructure. Millions of Pakistani citizens cannot easily query their own government's documents because they exist in linguistically inaccessible, unstructured forms. Alongside coursework, I've built structured mathematics foundations for ML, earned certifications across HackerRank, Harvard CS50P, freeCodeCamp, Kaggle, HP LIFE, ADBI and SoloLearn, and am working daily on PAKGOV-RAG, a bilingual Urdu-English retrieval-augmented generation system. My long-term goal is a fully funded MS in AI abroad, followed by a PhD at a top global AI research lab.

Research

Flagship Project

PAKGOV-RAG

A bilingual Urdu-English Retrieval-Augmented Generation engine for Pakistani government document infrastructure — covering FBR tax circulars, HEC education policy, NADRA identity procedures, SECP corporate filings, and Supreme Court judgments. The system combines hybrid dense + sparse retrieval (FAISS + BM25) with a QLoRA fine-tuned bilingual generation pipeline, evaluated with the RAGAS framework across faithfulness, answer relevance, context precision, and context recall.
