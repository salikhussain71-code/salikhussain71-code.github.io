Flagship project

PAKGOV-RAG

A bilingual Urdu-English retrieval-augmented generation engine for Pakistani government documents: FBR circulars, HEC policy, NADRA procedures, SECP filings and Supreme Court judgments. It combines hybrid dense and sparse retrieval (FAISS and BM25) with a QLoRA fine-tuned bilingual generator, evaluated with RAGAS on faithfulness, answer relevance, context precision and context recall.
