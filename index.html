PAKGOV-RAG

In progress

A bilingual Urdu-English retrieval-augmented generation system for Pakistani government and legal documents: ingestion, chunking, multilingual embeddings, BM25 plus dense retrieval, reciprocal rank fusion, grounded generation with citations, and failure analysis.

  1. IngestPDFs to text with PyMuPDF
  2. Chunk6,711 chunks with page and article metadata
  3. RetrieveBM25, dense (FAISS) and hybrid RRF, k = 60
  4. AnswerCited answers, or "insufficient evidence"

Measured results

One 50-question set shared by every retriever: 15 English, 15 Urdu, 20 mixed-language. Values as reported in my 30 September 2026 write-up; the evaluation files are in the repository. The set is small, so treat the numbers as indicative, not as a benchmark.

Limits, stated plainly

  • The evaluation set is small, so one retriever beating another by a few points may not hold on a larger set.
  • The corpus covers a limited set of official documents, and the system gives no legal advice.
  • Next: article-aware chunking, reranking, larger natural-question sets, broader government coverage.
